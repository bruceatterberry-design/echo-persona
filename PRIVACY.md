# Privacy Policy — Echo Persona

*Last updated: November 15, 2026*

Echo Persona is a personal, local digital assistant that runs on its owner's own Mac. It is built for private use by one household and is not a service for the public.

## What stays on your Mac
Everything Echo learns and remembers — conversations, memories, voice and face data, documents, and settings — is stored on the owner's own computer. Echo Persona has no servers of its own, no accounts, and no analytics. The developer does not receive, collect, or have access to any of your data.

## Google account access
If you choose to connect a Google account, Echo asks for one narrow permission: **`drive.file`**. It lets Echo see and change **only the files and folders Echo itself creates** (for example, an "AI Documents" folder). It cannot read your other Drive files, email, calendar, contacts, or photos.

- Echo uses this only to save documents you approve into your own Google Drive.
- The sign-in token is stored only on your Mac, readable only by your user account.
- You can disconnect at any time from Echo, or at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.

Echo's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

## Information that leaves your Mac
Echo works locally by default. Only these things may leave your Mac, and only when you use the feature:

- **Web searches:** when you ask Echo to look something up, only the search words are sent to a public search engine (for example DuckDuckGo or Bing). Echo does not send personal memories or conversations.
- **Web pages:** Echo fetches public pages you ask about, the way a browser does.
- **Optional helper services:** if you turn on an optional helper (such as a cloud AI service) for a specific task, the text for that task is sent to that service under its own terms. These are off unless you enable them, and Echo removes names of private individuals from document text first.

## Children and sensitive information
Echo is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them. Documents that mention students or other private individuals are handled on the owner's Mac and are de-identified before any optional helper sees them.

## Your control
Because the data lives on your Mac, you control it: view it, export it, or delete it at any time by using Echo's own settings or removing its data folder.
